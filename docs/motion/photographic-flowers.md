# Flower media and blooming coverage

The recipient renderer uses real, species-checked local photographs. The rejected procedural flowers do not render as a fallback. All images use contain sizing to retain their whole composition.

## Actual opening footage

- Alstroemeria: Pexels 29968086, first 32 seconds of the source, retimed to approximately seven seconds. Visually checked closed, intermediate, and open petals. Avoided the source's later cut to a different angle.
- Phalaenopsis: Eric Versaw, Pexels 4010613, full 12.14-second sequence retimed to approximately seven seconds. Visually checked bud to open spotted moth orchid.

Both clips are muted, locally hosted H.264, 960px wide, loaded only after the visitor starts a bloom. Pause, hidden-document pause, replay, skip, live reduced-motion preference and failure recovery preserve greeting access. Initial and final posters come from the same clip. These are recorded biological movement, not 3D reconstructions.

## Missing footage: explicitly not completed

Pink rose, peach rose, peony, cherry blossom and Lilium remain real photographs with a modest perspective/scale reveal. **That reveal is not petal blooming.** The user approved verified free footage with missing flowers flagged. No paid asset was bought. Two free rose candidates were visually rejected for insufficient opening. Peony/cherry Pixabay and Lilium Vecteezy leads were not integrated because a usable download and/or full visual/license acceptance was unavailable. No species was substituted to pretend seven-flower coverage.

## Rights and presentation

Source pages, authors, licenses, transformations, sizes and SHA256 hashes are in public/flowers/LICENSES.json. Pexels allows website use and adaptation without attribution under https://www.pexels.com/license/. Existing CC BY/BY-SA photographs keep their listed terms. Attribution is retained on the separate /credits/ page and in the public asset manifest. The celebration's source accordion, static-mode button and counter/title strip were removed as requested. Media files are local; recipients make no request to stock-media providers.

Detailed candidate/contact evidence is local under ignored artifacts/quality/asset-research. No generated or watermarked stock preview was adopted.
